THE WORLD OF NINJAGO

    • Lego Ninjago is a lego theme that saved the company of LEGO from terrible downfall.Due to some terrible economical descisions, the company was falling, and their rival,Megabloks, was rising. the only thing keeping the company at that point was Bionicle, another LEGO theme. So the company had to come up with a theme that kids around the world would like. That's when NINJAGO was born. the stories feature a group of six ninja fighting powerful enemies. I've seen the show personally and I think that NINJAGO is a mixture of emotion,humour,mystery and action. Ninjago recently celebrated its 15th aniversary
